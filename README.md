### Hi, I'm Mishkin 👋

I'm Co-Founder, CEO & Chief Scientist of [MaxGood.work](https://maxgood.work), an AI coaching platform. I build AI agents, and the software factories, evals and guardrails that let them ship real code. At MaxGood.work, coding agents build from approved plans, and independent AI reviewers, test suites and evals decide what ships.

#### Hooked on AI since grade 9

- **Grade 9:** a friend and I typed ELIZA into his Commodore VIC-20, and I was hooked.
- **Grade 11:** with another friend, I implemented back-propagation on his Mac Plus, a letter-recognition network built from an article in *Scientific American*. I added a variant of my own that learned from just a "yes" or "no". It won two awards at the regional science fair.
- **1991–93:** evolving artificial life: "turmites", cellular-automaton creatures that ate, moved, bred and mutated on a two-dimensional toroidal world, where survival was the only fitness test. I wrote the first version in Turbo Pascal on MS-DOS in 1991, then rewrote it for NeXTSTEP as HiddenWords, which I released as shareware. The real challenge was making it run fast.
- **Late 1990s:** natural-language processing at Troba, a startup. Our system found the key text on e-commerce web sites, analyzed its grammar, sentence structure and vocabulary, scored how well it would sell, and recommended improvements.
- **Today:** AI agents, evals and cognitive architectures, at MaxGood.work and in the open-source projects below.

#### Open source (this account)

- **[DAASH](https://github.com/MishkinBerteig/Distributed-Agile-Agentic-Swarm-Harness)**: the Distributed Agile Agentic Swarm Harness. It turns a vision into teams of AI agents, each with a Mission Judge, a Quality Judge, a Definition of Ready and a Definition of Done. Runs entirely on local open-weight models.
- **[Petacat](https://github.com/MishkinBerteig/Petacat)**: a port and re-architecture of Metacat (James Marshall), which built on Copycat (Melanie Mitchell), from Douglas Hofstadter's research on analogy-making. A lockstep replay harness proves the port faithful: 1,319 recorded runs, 50.6 million random decisions, 100% match.
- **[llm-router](https://github.com/MishkinBerteig/llm-router)**: one OpenAI-compatible endpoint that routes agents to local models by capability tier (amazing, great, fast, instant). A single Go file, standard library only.
- **[agent-critic](https://github.com/MishkinBerteig/agent-critic)**: grades an AI response on two separate axes, severity and quality, and never lets a model grade its own output.
